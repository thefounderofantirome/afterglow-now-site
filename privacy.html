Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Afterglow Interactive & Technology Limited develops and operates desktop applications, mobile applications, and browser extensions including Float, Chitchat, KitFerry, and Mark. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information across our software products and websites.

1. Data controller

Legal entity: Afterglow Interactive & Technology Limited (D-U-N-S: 374225078)
Registered office: Unit 909, Prosperity Millennia Plaza, 663 King's Road, Quarry Bay, Hong Kong
Contact email: support@afterglow.now

2. Information we process

  • Account identifiers such as your email address and authentication tokens when you sign in to cloud-synced apps.
  • User-saved items such as bookmarked subtitles, vocabulary cards, and display preferences synced to your account.
  • Subtitle text segments or lookup words submitted on demand to translation and dictionary APIs when you explicitly activate translation on a video in Float.
  • Billing, license, and subscription entitlement records processed through regulated payment providers (including Apple App Store, Google Play, Airwallex, or Stripe).
  • Support correspondence and safety reports submitted by users.

3. Local-first tools and per-video standby

Local desktop utilities such as Mark and KitFerry store schedules and workstation profiles directly on your device. In Float, newly opened videos start in standby with zero translation requests until you explicitly enable subtitles for that video. We never access unrelated browsing history, passwords, or third-party cookies.

4. How we use information

We process data strictly to deliver requested software features, synchronize account memberships across devices, prevent fraud or platform abuse, provide customer support, and comply with applicable legal and tax obligations. We never sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Retention and deletion

You may clear saved items or request complete account deletion at any time inside application settings or by emailing support@afterglow.now. Limited billing records are retained only as required by accounting, payment compliance, and applicable law.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data export, or deletion requests, contact support@afterglow.now or founder@afterglow.now.
