Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Afterglow Interactive & Technology Limited develops and operates desktop applications, mobile applications, and browser extensions including Float, Chitchat, KitFerry, and Mark. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information across our software products and websites.

1. Data controller

Legal entity: Afterglow Interactive & Technology Limited (D-U-N-S: 374225078)
Registered office: Unit 909, Prosperity Millennia Plaza, 663 King's Road, Quarry Bay, Hong Kong
Contact email: support@afterglow.now

2. Visiting this website

You can browse afterglow.now without creating an account. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The subtitle demo runs in your browser using a fixed example. We use no advertising cookies or analytics scripts on this site. GitHub’s privacy statement explains how GitHub handles information.

3. Using our software

  • Float. When you activate subtitles or open a video in the reader, Float requests captions from YouTube. Translation and dictionary services receive the text needed for your request. Depending on the feature, this can include a word, caption excerpts, the video title, and surrounding context. The browser extension stores preferences, saved vocabulary, and quotes in local browser storage.
  • Chitchat. The service uses your sign-in email to authenticate your account. Its hosting, matching, and moderation services process letters and messages to match participants, deliver conversations, and check content. Reports and block records are used to investigate abuse and enforce blocks. Leaving a conversation and requesting account deletion are separate actions.
  • KitFerry. KitFerry reads the files, application settings, and presets you select for migration. A backup or local network transfer copies them to the destination you choose.
  • Mark. Calendar entries, deadlines, and timer preferences are stored on your device.

4. Support and purchases

If you email us, our email provider processes your address, message, and any attachments so we can respond. For a purchase inquiry we may use an order reference, license information, and correspondence to investigate a charge or process a refund. This website has no checkout and collects no card details. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and your choices

We keep support and safety records for as long as needed to resolve requests, investigate reports, and meet legal obligations. Applicable accounting requirements may require us to retain billing records. You can clear Float extension data in your browser and manage KitFerry backup copies at the destination you selected. Chitchat accepts account deletion requests in settings.

To request access, correction, export, or deletion of information we hold, email support@afterglow.now with the product name and the information concerned. We may need to verify your relationship to the account before acting.
