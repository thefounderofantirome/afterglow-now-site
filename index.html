Software portfolio

These are the products we develop. For availability on a particular device, contact our team.

Float

iOS, Android, Browser Extension

Read YouTube captions alongside a translation, look up a word in its sentence, and follow the transcript as the video plays.

  • Timestamp alignment powered by semantic 1:1 matching across 12 languages
  • Turn on subtitles for a video or open it in the companion reader
  • Save vocabulary and quote cards locally in the browser extension

Chitchat

iOS & Android Mobile App

Write a short letter about a subject on your mind. Chitchat uses the meaning of the letter to introduce two people for a private text conversation.

  • Intent-driven pairing powered by semantic 1:1 matching
  • In-app reporting and blocking controls
  • Either participant can leave a conversation

KitFerry

Windows Desktop App

Selective workstation, application profile, and studio preset migration engine built for creators, technicians, and multi-PC operators moving environments cleanly between machines.

  • Granular discovery and transfer of creative tool presets, developer configs, and app profiles
  • Deterministic local verification before and after workstation handoff
  • Transfer selected settings in a backup file or over the local network

Mark.

Desktop app

A desktop calendar and deadline timer that pairs structured daily segments with an ambient sunlit wall that tracks elapsed focus time.

  • Month calendar navigation with keyboard-first scheduling and exact session persistence
  • Deadline-driven focus clock with built-in ambient audio deck and reduced-motion support
  • Local-first storage with native window controls on macOS and Windows
